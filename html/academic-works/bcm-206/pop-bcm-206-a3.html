    1 - Nature of DA

    • This Digital Artifact (DA) I have produced is an art tutorial in short form video format, with memes injected at each section of the video in hopes to attract the attention from audiences of the younger generations.

    • The editing style is heavily inspired by IGBLON (igobylotsofnames) on YouTube, where they use TTS to ramble in excessive details explaining what they do in their modded minecraft playthrough. I personally find their style amusing; it could be used in my DA with only a handful of tweaks. Hence, I picked theirs as the inspiration.

    3 - Production Process of DA

    • The production is roughly separated into few stages. First and the obvious, gathering clips; then, writing a script, and getting a TTS to speak it; and finally it is finding other visual assets from the internet all while editing everything together.


    • Since the tutorial teaches how to use Blender in a 2D illustration, I had to gather clips from Blender and CLIP STUDIO PAINT (The drawing app, abbreviated as CSP), the clips from Blender are recorded via OBS Studio; timelapses are natively exported from CSP. The final opening and closing clips were not recorded when the artwork was still in progress, they are recorded afterwards to make up the explanation parts


    • Then, the script was written on a notepad and sent to TTS websites, with US Joey as the voice generator. Which is also used by IGBLON in their videos. Some TTS websites have a generation limit. Therefore, I had to switch between several, or clear my current browsing session to generate more.


    • Finally, the meme sound effects are gathered from the meme soundboard called MyInstants, other meme video clips were gathered via YouTube and downloaded via a YouTube to Mp4 converter. And combined altogether in Adobe Premiere Pro.


    4 - Trouble I ran into when producing this DA

    • The production process was rather smooth, with one main exception: When I was trying to use an OGG audio track in Adobe Premiere Pro, it was abruptly declined because it was not in a supported format. To solve this problem, I first tried HandBrake and realized it can only transcode videos. Later, I went into the fifth layer of the internet to seek out a (seemingly) sketchy software, called Foobar2000, which sounds like a Robocop model or something.

    • Then, it wasn't over yet, as it turns out that Foobar was only a wrapper software, and I have to install a MP3 encoder too, which is called Lame Encoder (very lame situation indeed). I struggled as I could only find source codes and make files for it, but then I stumbled upon a precompiled release somewhere on the internet. After all that trouble, I could finally use Foobar2000 to use Lame to convert an OGG soundtrack into MP3 and put it to use in Adobe Premiere Pro. Such a very hectic installation experience indeed.

    5 - Personal opinion on producing this type of DA

    • Personally, I find this type of DA is sort of draining. First I thought it's mental drain is kinda similar to doing an illustration, but in an illustration, the steps in the workflow is very distinctive: First it starts with a draft; then you refine it into a line art by drawing over; paint the base colors below; and then do the shading; repeat the same with backgrounds either before or after the character. It is rather clear compared to editing this style of short form videos.

    • On the other hand, it is rather chaotic to edit the video, and there are much resources to gather from everywhere. Namely, the memes and especially what types of memes to insert, what and when. Whereas you can find references from just Twitter in an illustration. Not to mention images and audios should be royalty free, so that I can avoid getting a copyright strike.

    • But in the end, I suppose it is mainly because I was used to creating illustrations. It is not to say that illustrations aren't difficult. Rather, they feature a very high skill ceiling compared to video editing; you can always improve at every step. While in video editing, the entry barrier is considerably lower. Most of the time, creativity is only useful in the drafting phrase of an illustration; most visuals are set in stone and not much is subjected to change. In video editing, you need creative thoughts in almost every corner of the video just to make everything interesting.
