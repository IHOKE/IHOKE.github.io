    Autoethnographic Exploration of the Media Niche

    Introduction

    In the upcoming assignment, I will be exploring different digital illustrations on Twitter / X, and specifically what ACG elements engage more audiences.


    This topic is highly relevant to me, as I have already established a following on Twitter as a digital illustrator. And it would be a great interest to learn what contents do viewers (especially my followers) like to see on the platform, so as to create better contents and attract more followers.


    In this blog post, I will briefly go through how I plan to explore this topic, as well as my biases, epiphanies and academic sources used in the project.


    BIASES - ARTIST STATEMENT

    As an aspiring artist creating mainly art content, I always think that human art conveys more emotion than a generated art.


    While it is true that generative tools can compensate a creative mind that lacks skill; Investing time into a skill is part of the art.


    The skill we have reflects every aspect of the art we produce, and it is intrinsically human to struggle through rounds of failures; Before we can manage to create something astounding.


    It reflects us humans as a race, we have many hardships, but we stand our ground to face them.


    I hate to say it, but to some extent I agree "Struggling is Authenticity"


    RESEARCH DATA

    There are several potential sources of data for this digital ethnographic project, and I will name them below:


    • 1 - Questionaire Survey on My Twitter Followers

    • Because most of my followers are scattered across the globe, the best way to understand what my followers like on the platform is to ask them directly.

    • This survey will be voluntary in nature, with an giveaway event acting as both an incentive, and a way to directly engage my audiences.

    • While the audiences will be asked of what visual elements they engage the most, they may also be asked of how they buy merchandzies, this data may prove insightful should I wish to monetize my content in the future.

    • 2 - Content Review / Field Study on Other Illustrators on X

    • In this field study, I will be performing digital ethnography on X digital illustrators of similar niche, in an attempt to gather art posts with high engagement; analyze different visual themes and deduce a pattern on what audiences of illustrations like seeing on such contents.

    • In addition, I will also be doing a content review on my post history, taking note of what contents and visual elements attracts the most engagement. And hypothesize a theory to predict if a content is engaging.

    • 3 - Interviewing an Artist Friend

    • At last, I will be interviewing an artist friend who lives in America, whom have made a living by attending exhibitions and selling merchandizes designed by themselves.

    EPIPHANIES AND REFLECTIONS

    Due to the differences in content niches / operations I and the other illustrators create, I will bear in mind that some of the gathered data may not apply as accurately to me.


    This is especially true for the interview. As my artist friend in question mainly operate in participating conventions physically, while I mainly operate behind screens and mostly digital spaces; I will take note that their operations may not benefit me significantly.


    This also applies to the Field Study. Following trends blindly (such as drawing trending characters) has proven to be ineffective in the past; This is most likely that the trend contents are being saturated by other more competitive illustrators, and my followers expect things other than trends. I should acknowledge the differences of my own content niches and other illustrator, and realize they have their own audiences.


    I also understand that the time scope of this project is rather short, spanning across several weeks. This implies that I might not have the time to execute and carry out all the data collection as mentioned from the section above. And some plans may be cut out as to adjust to such limited time frame.


    PEER-REVIEWED SOURCES

    According to Richter et al. (2025), micro celebrities uses 4 main strategies to sustain an audience, this includes: Educating, Evaluating, Advising, Entertaining. Which essentially breaks down into "providing niche content / knowledge in a humble, personalized and entertaining way."


    The paper also mentions the Two-Step Flow Theory, which claims such niche creators act as a specialized opinion leaders for their small fanbase to follow. Which attracts a highly targeted community, and they are less effective in creating contents that appease the general public.


    The insights from this paper not only verify my epiphanies as stated before, but also provides a basic idea on how niche creators balance their content around these 4 major metrics. And shall be analyzed carefully throughout.
