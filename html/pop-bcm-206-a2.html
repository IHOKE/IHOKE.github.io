    3 - Presentation

    Due to the fact that my audiences are mainly composed of younger generations, I must adapt to their preferences in order to make my narrative more appealing.


    • No Generative Content

    • Undoubtedly, The majority of younger demographic appreciate the authenticity of an artist pouring efforts into creating art behind the scenes. As such, it is an undeniable fact that any use of generative content will lead to a severe backlash.

    • Nonetheless, I prefer to create without artificial assistance.

    • Short Form Content

    • It is not any news that Gen Z have lower attention span. To adopt to this attribute of theirs, I picked Short Form Content as my format; This format is commonly seen in platforms dominated by the same users, such as Tik tok or Youtube Shorts.

    • This implies the fact that such content is likely to be watched on mobile devices, where users are scrolling to kill time. It is therefore that this content is create in a vertical format, to be better fitted on a handheld device.

    • Not to mention, the process of creating an illustration is long and mundane to most, and is no match to an average viewer's attention span. As such, the clips shown in the video are sped up, as much as to 20 times to their original speed. It is critical to keep the content concise for picky viewers.

    • Crude Editing - Inspired by IGBLON

    Youtube Link



    • IGBLON (Also known as igobylotsofnames on Youtube) is the original creator that inspired the editing style of this content. In this style, memes are persistently injected into each section of the video to describe the most unremarkable details of the presentation. While this "brainrot" may be considered problematic to some, this exact trait of the style is what gains the attention of younger viewers.

    • Not only memes are constantly adding humorous remarks into my video, it is also worth noting that the editing style is notoriously crude, this is done with intent; Whereas to simulate the early days of the internet where the lack of editing skills in early Youtube videos are often laughed out of the office. Thus contributing to the lighthearted nature of the video.

    • Background Track

    • To add to the crammed, sped-up and chaotic pace of the video; And to better reflect to my followers, whom are fans of a game that I draw characters with. I used an original sound track from a game where I draw fan arts with. This is to further immerse viewers into a whimsical nature of the content.
