    Autoethnographic Critical Reflections on Content Generation and Platform Proliferation

    1 - Introduction

    During the production of the Digital Artifacts, I chose to produce tutorials on digital illustrations. These tutorials details the techniques I use when I create an illustration; And are highly technical: such as Blending modes, and using 3D models in the background.


    They are mainly short form contents, which are around 1-2 minutes. They are mostly videos with text laid on top with royalty free BGMs accompanying them, as I am not as confident to do voiceovers just yet.


    Such contents will be posted onto Twitter / X, IG and Facebook. With the first being the most active platform I use, and my followers on that platform are predominantly western.


    • 2 - Process of Digital Ethnography

    • Follower Survey

    • The primary source of digital ethnography is the Follower Survey I have gathered from my Twitter audiences. After the survey has concluded, there are 52 (53 - 1 duplicate) valid responses, and around 30 (52 - 23 negative responses) respondents said they are interested in art tutorials.


    • The finding states that anatomy is the most demanded topic amongst my followers (17 respondents). While it certainly is the highest count of all, I am not particularly phenomenal when drawing anatomy. And I find explaining such topic rather difficult; Not to mention such topics are very widely explored by other creators and likely saturated. As such, I will be picking a topic that I excel at and much less explored by the community: 3D backgrounds and blending modes.

    • Erotic Contents

    • The second part of the survey examines whether my audiences engages in erotic contents on the platform. While 17 respondents opted out this section, 35 respondents participated. From the gathered data, we can see that these participants are significantly attracted to alluring contents on the platform; And a considerable amount of them are interested in characters in suggestive / revealing clothing.


    • While it is certainly alluring to create erotic artifacts, they are hardly educational and it is difficult to express the thought process behind designing seductive outfits. As such, this topic shall not be considered.

    • Twitter Ethnography

    • Apart from the survey, I had also performed ethnography on tutorial contents on Twitter. After going through several sessions of different posts, it is clear to see that such contents can generate a considerable amount of engagements.

    • 3 - Generation Process and Difficulties

    • Use of Generative Tools

    • Being a human artist on the platform, it is safe to say that I will not be using AI to create such contents, doing so will definitely trigger a backlash. As such, this will translate to more time spent on creating contents.

    • The 4 Theories of Content Narrative

    • According to research papers from previous blog posts, there are mainly 4 narrative strategies that are critical in attracting engagements. While the tutorials are certainly entertaining, they are likely not entertaining to watch. To maintain a balance in between, I considered to include memes in the videos and even amusing sound effects. Doing so not only make my contents more entertaining to watch, they also contribute to my personal style as to discuss things more leisurely.

    • Since it is my first time creating tutorials, I have trouble on deducing what is tutorial worthy; The fact that I have not formally studied art theories also contribute to this issue. Despite this, I have managed to get some recordings from when I create 3D models, and timelapses when I create illustrations. I would need to plan out and critical points, and reference from other artists to aid me in the creation process of such contents.

    • Different Audiences

    • The final challenge is to realizing that my audiences on different platforms are significantly different, while my Twitter followers are mainly global and western, the ones on my IG and Facebook are predominantly Chinese speakers. As such, I will need to localize the texts in my contents with regards to whichever platform I post onto.

    • 4 - Conclusion

    • Being a content creator is no easy task, while I do have experience on that and had ran my Twitter account for almost 4 years now, it has became apparent to me that a consistent content schedule is extremely critical to growing a following.

    • When I was transitioning from the absence of a schedule to one, this is remarkably difficult to upkeep as I am never an organized person; I just do things the way I feel like it. As one might imagine, this is rather catastrophic as media platforms tend to punish creators who do not post consistently.

    • As such, I have learnt to outline my posting schedule using a calendar, to clearly organize what contents to post in the next month. Also, the schedule send features in Twitter and Meta is life saving, as they act as a buffer to post even when you are away.
