    Contextual Research Report

    1 - Introduction

    • Human attention is finite. As content creators, we need to lower the consumption costs our content to convince our users that they are worthy of their time; in return, this attention quality gives us more reach, exposure and engagement. While lowering consumption cost is crucial to capture users' focus to our content, the later allow creators to retain user attention.

    • These two is affected by various factors. Evidently, formats play a major role in contributing to the levels to both. However, they may also be affected by external factors that are less apparent creators, and often out our of their control; one example of this is algorithmic suppression.

    • In order to better capture and retain my followers' attention, I have conducted ethnographies and a survey. This research helps to understand how my chosen format and visual theme may affect attention quality, allowing me to propose ways to reduce their consumption costs.

    3 - Explore Key Epiphanies

    • In this project, I have chosen the following format a theme: Short form videos and Explicit materials, to explore how they affect attention quality, and see if they are subject to consumption cost. And elaborate my findings and epiphanies.


    • A - Short Form Videos
      Entertainment is Key to lower consumption cost and quality attention

    • According to Richter et al. (2025), there are 4 theories to creating content that retain viewers' attention: Educating, Evaluating, Advising, Entertaining. In this case study, I was first attempting to make an art tutorial. While tutorials are certainly educating, they are mostly static images containing technical information that are difficult to digest, thus making them hardly entertaining.

    • In addition, most art tutorials are only useful to viewers who are genuinely interested in improving their art. For those who are not an artist, they may find this information irrelevant to them, proving little to no motive to study them. In the unlikely event that they do, they can hardly understand the underlying theories as they are not acknowledgeable in the field. Hence contributing to the consumption cost.

    • While being entertaining is a general rule to creating engaging content according to Richter et al. (2025), it should also be noted that my audiences are mainly composed of younger generations, as supported by numerous reaction images as replies on my Twitter, and general skepticism towards AI in creative context.

    • It is a well known fact that Gen Zs have a lower attention span. In order to adopt into this attribute of theirs, not only should the tutorial be made entertaining, they should also be fast paced and short form to adopt into their consumption culture. The usage of memes will also greatly aid in attracting their attention.

    • After looking for other formats that satisfy the above requirements, I have found Short form videos to be the most suitable for this purpose, as they allow more opportunities to inject humorous elements in between. One sample of this is the creator igobylotsofnames (a.k.a. IGBLON) on YouTube. While they posts long videos, they specialize in allocating numerous memes into their content, fitting into the Gen Z narrative.

    • While their content could span into hours long, their viewership count reaches millions consistently without using short form video as a format. This pattern suggests that this editing style is capable of attracting high quality attention. Even when the content is long and the audience is made up of young viewership, they stay and watch the content long enough that the algorithm promotes it to other viewers.

    • As a result, I took this editing style as a reference and adopted it to a short form video format. While keeping the distinctive style of IGBLON's video helps attract quality attention, I also implemented other measures to to lower consumption costs, such as: vertical aspect ratio so that it fits better on a mobile devices; made sure the video length is no longer than several minutes.

    • While TikTok is undoubtedly the best platform to post such content onto, due to the nature of it being a shorts platform. I only have access to its Chinese counter part: DouYin. Therefore, the content is only posted onto X Twitter, Youtube and Reddit.

    • B - Explicit Materials
      Implicit Materials for lower Consumption Cost; Explicit materials for higher Attention Quality

    • In this article, I will define Implicit materials and Explicit materials as follow:

    • Implicit materials may be suggestive, but does not depict sensitive body parts.
    • Explicit materials always depict sensitive body parts.

    • According to data gathered from survey on my Twitter followers, participants demonstrated strong willingness to seek explicit materials on the platform. While one may come to the conclusion this will directly translate into engagement, my ethnography on Twitter has proven otherwise: While explicit contents generate great amount of engagement (>10k likes) on accounts that has massive following count (>50k followers, see examples) they generate much less engagement when produced by lesser content creators (<50k followers.)

    • This realization contradicts to the fact that Twitter is known for its prominent sexual themes. I first attempted to explain this with Twitter's purpose as a social media platform. Compared to websites dedicated to distributing explicit content, Twitter could potentially have positioned itself into an art gallery instead. As such, users give more endorsement to artistic expressions, and implicit materials fulfill that purpose better than explicit materials. Otherwise, they would have simply visited a dedicated adult website.

    • Not only do they enjoy artistic intent from such materials on X, seeing implicit materials on the platform gives room for fantasy. Whereas an adult websites already fulfill the purpose in supplying explicit materials. As a result, implicit materials on X generates more engagement than explicit materials.

    • While this may help explain such question, AuditSocials (2026) explained that accounts that posts frequent adult content may not be recommended to non-followers, restricting them from cold discovery and effectively suppressing their identity under the algorithm. Naturally, this does not affect famous NSFW artists nearly as much, as their reach within their massive fanbase mitigate the need of cold discovery. However, this does not mean that lessor creators stand no chance in monetizing explicit content.

    • As algorithmic suppression presents major risks to creator who wishes to monetize explicit content, some illustrators have adopted to posting implicit materials exclusively on X, an separate them apart from their monetization platforms, such as Patreon, Fanbox and Booth. Under this practise, implicit materials can help avoid algorithmic suppression, lowering consumption cost to sexual contents on Twitter; explicit materials acts as the ending monetization goal, converting click rates into high quality attention.
